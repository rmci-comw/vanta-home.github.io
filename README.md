# VANTA HOME

**Light Your Space. Live Your Mood.**

VANTA HOME is a premium lighting storefront concept with a room-first, editorial shopping experience.

## V2 Direction

This version intentionally follows the visual language of the approved VANTA HOME reference: dark luxury hero, warm cream category band, round category photography, a bedroom editorial split, a real-life benefits panel, curated products, mood shopping, support, and a dark editorial footer.

## Image Rules

Product photography is always a **single independent photograph per product card**. Images are kept separate from HTML controls and are displayed in square frames with centered proportions; no product card screenshot is used as product photography.

The homepage hero and bedroom editorial are dedicated photography assets. Product/category photographs use direct image URLs from free-photo sources and should be rechecked against current source licenses before a commercial launch.
